E-waste is one of the world's fastest-growing waste streams. The world produced a record 62 million tonnes of it in 2022.

The 2021 UK rules make manufacturers supply spare parts for some appliances, like washing machines, for years after they stop selling them.

Phones, laptops and tablets are not covered. Whether the rules should stretch to them is the open question.
